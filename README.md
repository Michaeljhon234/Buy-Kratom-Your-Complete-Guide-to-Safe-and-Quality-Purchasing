- **Harvesting methods**: Traditional hand-picking versus commercial harvesting affects quality
- **Processing techniques**: Drying and fermentation methods alter the final product
- **Storage conditions**: Proper storage preserves alkaloid potency

## Storage and Freshness: Protecting Your Kratom Purchase

After you buy kratom, proper storage maintains potency and prevents degradation. Kratom's alkaloids remain stable under correct conditions but degrade when exposed to certain environmental factors.

### Optimal Storage Conditions

**Temperature**: Store kratom in cool, stable environments. Room temperature (68-72°F) works well. Avoid:
- Direct heat sources
- Locations with temperature fluctuations
- Extremely cold environments (freezing isn't necessary)

**Light exposure**: UV light degrades alkaloids over time. Store kratom:
- In opaque containers
- Away from windows and direct sunlight
- In dark cabinets or drawers

**Moisture**: Humidity causes mold growth and alkaloid breakdown. Protect kratom by:
- Using airtight containers
- Including desiccant packets in storage
- Avoiding bathroom storage
- Keeping away from kitchen steam and moisture

**Air exposure**: Oxygen gradually oxidizes alkaloids. Minimize exposure through:
- Vacuum-sealed bags for long-term storage
- Airtight containers with minimal headspace
- Removing air before sealing bags

### Container Recommendations

**Best storage containers:**
- Mason jars with tight-fitting lids
- Vacuum-sealed bags
- Mylar bags with zip seals
- Food-grade plastic containers with airtight seals
- Original vendor packaging (if properly sealed)

**Label your storage** with:
- Strain name and vein color
- Purchase date
- Vendor name
- Batch number (if available)
- Expiration date

### Shelf Life Expectations

Properly stored kratom maintains potency for:
- **Powder**: 1-3 months (good), 3-12 months (acceptable), 12+ months (declining)
- **Capsules**: Similar to powder, though capsule material may degrade
- **Extracts**: Often longer shelf life due to concentration

**Signs of kratom degradation:**
- Faded or changed color
- Weakened aroma
- Clumping or moisture presence
- Reduced effects at normal doses
- Musty or off odors
